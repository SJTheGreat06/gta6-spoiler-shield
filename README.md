# 🛡️ GTA VI Spoiler Shield

**A uBlock Origin filter list for everyone who can't play GTA VI yet.** If you're a PC player, you're waiting on the PC release, or you're just not ready to see anything, subscribe and the game stops showing up on your internet until you've played it.

## ⚡ Subscribe (30 seconds)

1. Click the uBlock Origin icon, then the ⚙️ gear (Dashboard), then the **Filter lists** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom, tick **Import…** and paste this link:
   ```
   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SJTheGreat06/gta6-spoiler-shield/main/gta6-spoilers.txt
   ```
3. Click **Apply changes** at the top.

You'll see **GTA VI Spoiler Shield** in the list under **Custom**. ✅

### One-click shortcut

👉 **[Subscribe in uBlock Origin](https://subscribe.adblockplus.org/?location=https%3A%2F%2Fraw.githubusercontent.com%2FSJTheGreat06%2Fgta6-spoiler-shield%2Fmain%2Fgta6-spoilers.txt&title=GTA%20VI%20Spoiler%20Shield)**

Click it **on this GitHub page**. uBlock Origin catches the click and asks you to confirm. Don't copy it into your address bar: the old Adblock Plus subscribe site it points to no longer exists, so you'll get "Server Not Found". If that happens, use the steps above.

The list checks for updates every 12 hours, so new leak sites get blocked as they're added.

## What it blocks

| Where | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Any website** | Any page whose web address mentions GTA 6 is blocked before it loads. That includes Google and YouTube searches like `gta 6 ending` and article links like `.../grand-theft-auto-vi-review`. |
| **YouTube** | Videos on the home page, in search, in the sidebar, in Shorts, in comments, in community posts and in notifications. A video page you land on anyway is hidden too. |
| **Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo** | Results, Top Stories, "People also ask" and search suggestions. |
| **Reddit** | r/GTA6, r/GTA, r/rockstar and r/GamingLeaksAndRumours are blocked, and matching posts and comments are hidden everywhere else on Reddit. |
| **X / Twitter** | Tweets, replies and trends. |
| **Instagram, Facebook, Threads, Bluesky, TikTok, Twitch, Kick, Steam, Discord (browser), 9GAG** | Posts, cards, chat messages and comments. |
| **Gaming news** | IGN, GameSpot, Kotaku, Polygon, PC Gamer, Eurogamer, GamesRadar, TheGamer, Game Rant, VGC, The Verge. |
| **Rockstar's own sites** | rockstargames.com, the Newswire, GTAForums and GTABase are blocked outright. |

**Keywords caught** (upper or lower case doesn't matter): `GTA 6`, `GTA VI`, `GTA6`, `GTAVI`, `#GTA6`, `GTA: VI`, `Grand Theft Auto VI/6`, `Leonida`, `Vice City`, `Jason & Lucia`, `Lucia Caminos`, `Jason Duval`, `Rockstar Games`

**Not caught:** `GTA 5`, `GTA V`, `GTA Online` and lookalikes such as `gtavideos` or `GTA 60fps`.

> ⚠️ Old GTA: Vice City content and Rockstar's other games get hidden too. That's deliberate: it's the price of staying spoiler-free.

## Which browsers it works in

| Browser / blocker | Works? |
|---|---|
| **uBlock Origin** on Firefox, Firefox Android, Edge, Opera, Brave | ✅ Yes. This is what the list is written for. |
| **uBlock Origin Lite** (Chrome) | ⚠️ Only partly. Lite can't subscribe to custom lists. |
| **Brave Shields** (custom filter lists) | 🤷 Untested. Brave supports most uBlock syntax. |
| **AdGuard** | 🤷 Untested. |

**On Chrome?** Chrome no longer allows full uBlock Origin, so for this week the simplest fix is Firefox with uBlock Origin.

## 📱 Things no filter list can protect you from

- **Phone apps.** Add muted words (`GTA 6`, `GTA VI`, `GTA6`, `Rockstar`) in the X, Instagram, Threads, Reddit and YouTube apps.
- **Discord.** Link previews and chat in the desktop app aren't filtered. Mute your gaming servers.
- **Windows.** Turn off Widgets under Settings → Personalization → Taskbar, because its news feed will be full of GTA.
- **That one friend.** Six stars. Interpol. You know what to do.
